# Snívať — Product

What Snívať is, who it's for, and why people return.

This document defines the **product**. For how to build it, see `AGENTS.md`.

---

## Mission

Snívať is a community where people grow together.

It is **not** a job board.
It is **not** a networking website.
It is **not** LinkedIn for Nepal.

People join Snívať to:

- Build in public
- Share progress
- Receive honest feedback
- Find collaborators
- Discover opportunities through real interaction
- Document both success and failure

The journey is the content.

## Product Philosophy

**Tagline:** Grow in public. Find your people. Build what's next.

Core beliefs:

- Growth is a shared experience, not a solo grind
- The platform should feel like a community, not a corporate tool
- Discovery over application — people find opportunities through browsing and conversation, not just filling forms
- Profiles are living portfolios built through activity, not static resumes
- Connections form through real interaction, not one-click networking
- Curiosity-driven, not fear-driven — people return because they WANT to, not because they have to

The journey IS the content.

### What makes people return

- Curiosity about what someone posted today
- Real notifications — comments, applications, messages — not empty vanity metrics
- A feed that changes every visit because the community is active
- A reason to check in — did someone reply? did someone post something interesting?

Retention comes from product quality. People stay because they genuinely like the platform — not because they were pushed there.

## Product Priorities

Always prioritize, in order:

1. User experience
2. Reliability
3. Community
4. Performance
5. Appearance

Working software beats visual polish.

---

## Community First

Jobs are important. Community is more important.

The platform should still feel valuable even if no one is hiring.

People should return because:

- Someone replied
- Someone shared progress
- Someone built something interesting
- Someone asked for help

Not because they received another meaningless notification.

---

## Non-negotiables

Never add:

- Fake users
- Fake likes
- Fake comments
- Fake jobs
- Fake statistics
- Fake notifications

Everything should represent real activity. If something is empty, show that it is empty. **Never fake growth.**

Only real users. Only real growth. Quality over quantity in everything.

## Growth Context

**Funnel:** YouTube/TikTok Shorts → Discord Community → Snívať

- YouTube/TikTok: Content creator documents growth journey, attracts audience
- Discord: Community hub where members interact, collaborate
- Snívať: The product the community actually uses

### Phases

- **Phase 1 — YouTube (done):** Channel established, audience exists.
- **Phase 2 — Dev + Face (current):** Building Snívať while face creators produce content and grow the channels. The creators promote the channels, Discord, and Snívať together.
- **Phase 3 — Discord as filter:** Discord servers serve as natural selection processes. Not everyone who joins Discord will use Snívať — and that's intentional. The ones who build, earn, and prove themselves move to Snívať. This ensures quality users, not just warm bodies.
- **Phase 4 — Reclaim:** As the channels and Snívať grow, both Discord communities become fully owned as Snívať communities. The funnel completes.

**Content strategy:**
- Shorts-focused (YouTube Shorts + TikTok)
- Primary language: Nepali
- Face creator should be someone real, relatable, slightly ahead of the audience
- Content is about the creator's actual growth journey — not tutorials, not influencer lifestyle
- Snívať should be woven into the content naturally, not forced as a sponsorship

**Key insight:** Community and founder visibility help people discover Snívať, but retention must come from product quality.

### V1 is permanent

Do NOT suggest creating V2, V4, or any rewrite. V1 is the only version that matters. Restarting destroys momentum.
